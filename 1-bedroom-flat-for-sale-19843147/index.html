Description

Set within the heart of the highly regarded Kings Barton development, this beautifully presented one bedroom apartment offers contemporary living in a thriving and well connected Winchester location. With an increasing range of local amenities on the doorstep and convenient access to Winchester's historic city centre and mainline railway station, the property is perfectly suited to first-time buyers, professionals, or those seeking a stylish and low maintenance home.

The accommodation is thoughtfully designed and finished with a modern, sleek aesthetic throughout. An inviting entrance hall provides useful built in storage, leading to a spacious bathroom and a light filled double bedroom. The heart of the home is the impressive open plan reception room, offering generous space for both living and dining. The contemporary kitchen is fitted with an excellent range of cabinetry and integrated appliances, creating a practical yet stylish environment for everyday living and entertaining.

Double doors open onto a well proportioned private balcony, providing the perfect setting for al fresco dining, entertaining guests, or simply enjoying a morning coffee in the sunshine.

Externally, residents benefit from a secure bicycle store and two allocated parking spaces, completing this attractive home within a modern and well maintained apartment block.

Combining contemporary comfort with an excellent location, this superb apartment presents an ideal opportunity to enjoy all that the sought after Kings Barton community has to offer.
